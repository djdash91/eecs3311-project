## Collaboration log

Add an entry when an interaction changed the project in a way worth explaining: a design decision, a bug the AI found or caused, code you rejected or rewrote. Aim for quality over quantity; about 8 to 12 good entries tell the story.

The starter code was itself produced with AI help. Log that as your first entry, and say which parts you reviewed and changed.

### Entry template

**Date:**

**Task:** What were you trying to accomplish?

**Input or instruction:** What did you ask the AI to do? Paste or summarise the prompt, and note which design documents you gave it.

**AI contribution:** What did it generate, suggest, change or diagnose?

**Human contribution:** What did you review, correct, redesign, reject or improve? Be specific, for example "it skipped the busy check in undoLastFix; I added it and a test".

**Outcome:** What ended up in the project, and in which files?
